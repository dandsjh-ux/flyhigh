How to play

  • 🎯 Word Gates appear in the sky. Read the question and fly through the ring with the right answer.
  • 📻 ATC gives you instructions. Follow them for FlightPoints, and press 1–3 to pick the correct readback for a bonus.
  • 🗺️ Missions: fly to airports, deliver cargo, visit landmarks, rescue boats and race through rings.
  • 🛬 Landing: slow down, fly through the green rings, and touch down gently on the runway. Then press H for the hangar.
  • ✈️ Spend FlightPoints in the hangar on new planes, upgrades and paint — from the little Sparrow all the way to the Thunderbird jet!
  • ⏩ Cruise mode: on a long trip, point your plane at the mission marker (or press K). The autopilot flies you there fast with movie-style cameras, and gives you control back 2 km before you arrive.
  • 🌙 Day and night: the sun sets and the stars come out. At night, follow the bright runway lights. The flashing lights point to the runway, and the four PAPI lights show two red and two white when you are on the right slope. Night landings and night missions give bonus FP!
  • 🪂 New missions: hospital flights against the clock, parachute drops, water bombing a forest fire, sightseeing tours, and follow-the-leader formation flying.
  • 🌦️ After you become a Junior Pilot, the weather changes: strong wind pushes your plane, rain makes it hard to see, and thunderstorms (red on your radar) shake the plane and can hit you with lightning, thick fog hides everything, and low clouds turn the world white. Follow ATC to fly around storms!
  • ⭐ 15 golden treasure stars are hidden around the islands. Can you find them all?
